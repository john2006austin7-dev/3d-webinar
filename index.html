Frequently Asked Questions

Do I really need to post every day? ▼
No. Quality over quantity. We focus on creating 3-4 high-impact posts per week that genuinely resonate with your audience rather than daily volume.
How long does it take to see results? ▼
Most clients see meaningful traction within 6-8 weeks. Profile engagement increases happen faster, while pipeline impact takes 12+ weeks to fully materialize.
What if I don't have time to create content? ▼
That's exactly why the Management service exists. We handle the entire creation and publishing process. You just need 15 minutes per week to align on direction.
Is this just about getting more followers? ▼
No. Followers are a vanity metric. We focus on building authority, attracting qualified leads, and creating genuine business opportunities.
What's included in the free audit? ▼
You get a personalized video breakdown of your profile, positioning audit, content strategy review, growth opportunities, personal action plan, and a 90-day roadmap.
Do you guarantee results? ▼
We guarantee effort and strategy. LinkedIn's algorithm changes constantly. We adapt and optimize, but can't promise specific metrics or outcomes.
Can you help with personal brands and B2B? ▼
Yes, we specialize in executive personal brands primarily, but have worked with B2B companies and thought leaders across industries.
